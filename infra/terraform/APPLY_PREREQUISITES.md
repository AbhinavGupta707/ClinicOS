3. Inventory and back up every existing SSE-S3 state. Review the saved plan's phase/resource inventory, account/region, current monthly-cost estimate, public-access/IAM/KMS policy checks, replacements, and deletion/retention consequences. `foundation` must show zero NAT, interface endpoints, RDS, and cache; only advance phases deliberately.
